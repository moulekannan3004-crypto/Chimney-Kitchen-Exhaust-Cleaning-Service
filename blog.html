💡 Official Answer & Cleaning Schedule:

NFPA 96 Table 11.4 sets clear mandatory cleaning frequencies based on cooking volume and fuel type:

  • Monthly: Solid fuel operations (wood-burning, charcoal, heavy charbroiling).
  • Quarterly: High-volume commercial kitchens (24h diners, fast-food, wok stations).
  • Semi-Annually: Moderate-volume cooking (family style, hotel banquet halls).
  • Annually: Low-volume operations (churches, seasonal camps, senior centers).
💡 Official Answer:
