🛠️ Diagnostic & Repair Guide

Common Chimney & Exhaust Issues We Solve

From low suction and loud motor vibration to electrical power trips and auto-clean sensor errors, our certified technicians diagnose and repair all chimney models.

Issue 01

Low Suction / Smoke Escaping

Symptoms: Fumes linger in kitchen, paper test fails, oil drips from hood.

Cause & Fix: Caused by oil-saturated baffle filters or choked duct piping. We perform ultrasonic chemical filter soaking, riser pipe unclogging, and damper calibration to restore 100% airflow suction.

Issue 02

Abnormal Sound & Vibration

Symptoms: Loud rattling, high-pitched screeching, canopy shaking.

Cause & Fix: Uneven heavy grease buildup on fan impeller blades unbalances rotation. We scrape impeller blades down to bare metal, align motor shafts, and lubricate sealed bearings.

Issue 03

Chimney Not Turning On / Power Trip

Symptoms: Touch panel dead, circuit breaker trips on motor start.

Cause & Fix: Oil seepage into motor wiring causes electrical short circuits or blown capacitors. We replace motor capacitors, clean PCB touch control boards, and rewire sealed junction boxes.

Issue 04

Auto-Clean Heating Failure

Symptoms: Auto-clean light blinks, grease collector tray stays empty.

Cause & Fix: Heating element coil failure or clogged internal oil drain channels. We test thermal heating elements, clear internal drain spouts, and replace oil collector trays.

Issue 05

Heavy Oil Dripping & Foul Odor

Symptoms: Rancid grease smell, oil dripping onto burners during cooking.

Cause & Fix: Saturated mesh filters and carbon odor pad exhaustion. We provide ultrasonic dip tank filter cleaning, replacement baffle filters, and active carbon pad refills.

Issue 06

Unresponsive Touch / Switch Panel

Symptoms: Glass touch keys stick, speed levels fail to change.

Cause & Fix: Oil vapor condensation behind glass touch panels or worn mechanical push buttons. We install new capacitive touch sensor kits and water-resistant push switches.
